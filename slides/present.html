/deploy triage · before it writes a line

Four decisions only you can make

1 · What counts as urgent?

Blood pressure, glucose, and the words that are always urgent.

Northline chose 180/110 · glucose under 70 or over 300
2 · Messages that are not clinical

Admin staff, an approved auto-reply, or hold for the morning.

Northline chose admin staff
3 · Urgent at 1:48 am

Say call 911 and page the on-call nurse, say 911 only, or queue it.

Northline chose 911 and page
4 · "Don't tell the doctor"

Do you honour it? What is the patient told?

Northline chose no

What just happened
  • You didn't automate the bottleneck. You moved it.Escalations 300 to 2,400 a week. Nurse response 4 hours to 31. The agent did its job.
  • A tuning parameter can be a strategy decision in disguise.180/110 or 190/115 decides which patients do not get a call.
  • The same AI lets you respond in weeks.A quarter of logs read, a problem found, a tested triage layer deployed, in seventeen minutes.
  • A fast fix still gets tested like a clinical decision.Fifteen texts, a nurse's key, and a miss you could point at before any patient met it.
  • The business model has to pay for what the AI surfaces.$25 per patient per month, flat. Eight times the escalations. The same fee.
