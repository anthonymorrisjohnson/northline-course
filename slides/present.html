/deploy triage · before it writes a line

Four decisions only you can make

1 · What counts as urgent?

Blood pressure, glucose, and the words that are always urgent.

Northline chose 180/110 · glucose under 70 or over 300
2 · Messages that are not clinical

Admin staff, an approved auto-reply, or hold for the morning.

Northline chose admin staff
3 · Urgent at 1:48 am

Say call 911 and page the on-call nurse, say 911 only, or queue it.

Northline chose 911 and page
4 · "Don't tell the doctor"

Do you honour it? What is the patient told?

Northline chose no
